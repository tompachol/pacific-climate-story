# On the Edge of Disappearing 🌊

**An interactive data-visualisation project exploring climate vulnerability and resilience across Pacific Island countries and territories.**

![On the Edge of Disappearing]

🔗 **Live project:** https://pacific-climate-story-xhobatzdh-tompach.vercel.app/

## About

This project explores climate vulnerability and resilience across Pacific Island countries and territories. It combines data on the share of people living in low-elevation coastal zones, reported disaster impacts over time, annual sea-level anomalies, and progress in renewable energy.

Users can explore a coastal-exposure map, compare reported disaster impacts between countries, discover patterns in sea-level variation by location and year, and examine renewable-energy indicators.

The project uses **Svelte and D3.js**, with SVG-based visualisations, interactive tooltips, colour scales, and scroll-triggered animations to make complex environmental data more accessible and engaging.

The story highlights how climate change is already affecting Pacific communities. Population concentration near sea level, changing ocean conditions, and severe disasters create immediate local risks, while renewable energy offers a pathway toward greater energy independence, reduced reliance on imported fossil fuels, and long-term resilience.
